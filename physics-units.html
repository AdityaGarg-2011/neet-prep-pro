📖 NCERT Notes

Physical Quantity

A physical quantity is a measurable quantity expressed by a numerical value and a unit.

Physical Quantity = Numerical Value × Unit

Fundamental Quantities

  • Length → metre (m)
  • Mass → kilogram (kg)
  • Time → second (s)
  • Electric current → ampere (A)
  • Temperature → kelvin (K)
  • Amount of substance → mole (mol)
  • Luminous intensity → candela (cd)

Measurement Errors

Measurements can contain errors because of limitations of instruments and experimental conditions.

Significant Figures

Significant figures indicate the precision of a measured quantity.
